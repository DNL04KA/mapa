## Description

```
MAPA is a world map you colour in yourself.

Mark the countries you've been to, the one you live in and the ones you
want to see. The map keeps score: percent of the world, countries,
continents.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• 201 countries and 899 cities — from Russia to Singapore, Monaco and the
  Seychelles.
• A route across a country builds itself: the main cities, days for each,
  photographs of the places worth walking to.
• Over a thousand landmarks with photographs from Wikidata and Wikimedia
  Commons, each credited to its author with its licence.
• Your own map: four canvases and two palettes, from a night sky to warm
  paper. Countries are painted in the colours of their flags.
• An image and a video for stories — one tap, with your numbers on it.
• Search by country and city: Cologne is found as "Köln" and as "Кёльн".

THE HONEST PART

Nothing is collected. Marks, routes and photos live on the phone, no
account is needed and there is no sign-up. Photographs are fetched from
Wikimedia Commons and kept on the device — the map works on a trip with
no connection.

Where we have no data, it says so: "no description yet". There are no
invented landmarks in this app and there never will be.
```

## Age rating

4+ — no user content, no outbound links, no advertising. Once shared
routes and reviews arrive, the rating and the user-content questionnaire
have to be revisited.

## App Privacy

"Data Not Collected". This matches `MAPA/Resources/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`:
no tracking, and the only required-reason API in use is `UserDefaults`
(CA92.1).
